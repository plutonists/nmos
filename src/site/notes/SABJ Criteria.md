Hellos! This is the <span style="color:#ffc107">Stellar,</span> <span style="color:#00c853">Amazing,</span> <span style="color:#2979ff">Basic,</span> <span style="color:#ff3366">& Junk</span> (SABJ) Criteria, which I'll use to rank websites, software, and other stuff!
## About the Criteria
The SABJ Criteria is very simple. It operates on a <span style="color:#00c853">points-based system</span>. The more points a website/software gets depending on its category, the higher up it goes in ranking. However, for anything to be ranked at all, it must get past the <span style="color:#ff3366">Warden</span>.

### Who's the <span style="color:#ff3366">Warden</span>?
You should not be asking "Who", but "What". The Warden is an entity that safeguards the NMOS database, and throws certain stuff into the <span style="color:#ff3366">Junkyard.</span> My Warden acts as the filter, and brings me everything deemed good enough to be ranked above <span style="color:#ff3366">Junk.</span>

#### The Warden checks for the following:
> [!danger] Warden's Standards
> - **Basic Functionality:** Does it fulfill the expected? Is its usage agonizing?
>- **Advertisements & Safety:** How intrusive are the ads? Are the ads NSFW or malicious? Are the ads completely unmanageable even with adblocker turned on? Does it require you to turn off your adblocker? Is it malicious?
>  - **Paywall & Privacy**: Does the website have an unjustifiable paywall? Does it require you to signup with an account and hand over some of your information? Is there anything privacy-compromising even with proper tools (like VPN)? 

Failing **one** of these standards will get the website/software thrown into the Junkyard, no matter how good it was in other stuff, and thus <span style="color:#ff3366">recommended to avoid.</span> There will be a note about what exactly it failed, so you can watch out for it, if you want to try at your own risk.
